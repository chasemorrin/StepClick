Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email chaseappsupport@gmail.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a few days. It helps to mention your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I make a pattern?

Tap any box to cycle it from off to click to accent. Use the minus and plus buttons under Boxes to change how many steps the pattern has, and the tempo slider or buttons to set the speed. All Clicks turns every box on, and All Off clears them.

How do I save a pattern?

Tap Presets, type a name under "Save current pattern," and tap Save. Tap a saved pattern to load it, or swipe left on it to delete it.

Can I play along with music from another app?

Yes. Start your music first, then press Play in Step Click. Both play together.

Does it keep playing when my screen locks?

Yes. While it plays, you can pause and change the tempo from the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island. If those controls don't appear, check Settings › Step Click and make sure Live Activities is turned on.

It stopped when I got a call.

That's on purpose. Calls, alarms and Siri stop the metronome so it doesn't play over them. Press Play again when you're done.

Will I lose my presets?

Presets are saved on your iPhone. They stay through updates, but deleting the app deletes them.
