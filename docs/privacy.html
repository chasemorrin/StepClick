Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Short version: Step Click doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. Step Click has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app doesn't connect to any server run by the developer.

Information stored on your device

Your saved patterns (presets), tempo and sound choices are stored only on your iPhone so the app can remember them. They're never sent to the developer or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Apple

Apple may give developers general, anonymous information such as download counts and crash reports, depending on your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements). This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and doesn't identify you to the developer.

Children

Step Click is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
